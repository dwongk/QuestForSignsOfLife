Google Maps Photo Harvester v7.0

One click harvests your contribution photos and review captions, then syncs with your catalog.

How to Use

  1. Drag this button to your browser’s bookmarks bar:

    📸 Harvest Photos + Reviews (v7.0)

    Shortcut: Press Cmd+Shift+B (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) to show your bookmarks bar.

  2. Visit your Google Maps profile photos page:
    https://www.google.com/maps/contrib/109844961042142282833/photos
  3. Click the 📸 Harvest Photos + Reviews (v7.0) bookmark. It will automatically:
    • Scroll and extract all your photos from the Photos tab
    • Switch to the Reviews tab and load all your reviews
    • Match each review to the photo with the closest location and add it as a caption
    • Download maps_contributions.json

    This takes 30–60 seconds. Watch the browser console for progress logs.

  4. In terminal, run: 
    python3 manage.py import-maps ~/Downloads/maps_contributions.json
    Existing photos are matched and updated; new ones are added. No duplicates created.
